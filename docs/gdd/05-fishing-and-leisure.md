# 05 — Fishing and Leisure

> **Status: PROPOSED** — Canonical = approved direction; Proposed = hypothesis to validate; Open = undecided. Numeric examples are not final balancing.

## Design role
Fishing is Bapak's signature pleasure, a potential cash source, a mastery loop and a social hub. **The central game cannot depend on fishing alone for retention.** The family and logistics should remain interesting even if fishing were abstracted.

## Candidate fishing loop
Travel to pond → choose rod, bait, spot → cast → read bite cues → react with skill-based minigame → catch or lose fish → decide sell/keep/display/share → pursue goals. Environmental variations (weather, time, pond, fish behavior) may change tactics.

## Feedback and rewards
Immediate: bite tension, catch animation, personal best. Mid-term: equipment, new locations, local contests, trophies. Long-term: pond ownership, aquaculture, fishing-related social reputation.

## Anti-grind
Make daily fishing optional. Earnings from fishing should not trivially dominate every other strategy, or players will have no reason to invest in logistics/social systems. Avoid paid randomized loot or gambling mechanisms.

## Divorce-to-fishing sandbox route (accepted direction)
The creator wants possible household collapse/divorce to unlock or transition into an ironic **full-fishing sandbox**. This is a consequence branch, not a required route to access enjoyable leisure, and its implementation is not yet specified. See [Risk Economy §15](15-risk-economy-and-neighbor-exploitation.md). The regular household-centered fishing loop remains intact.

## Leisure beyond fishing
Warung kopi gossip, visiting neighbors, family outings, small repair jobs, local events and other hobbies can compete for reclaimed hours. Ideally other activities improve the management game through relationships or opportunities.

## Original rhythm
Morning school/factory commute, daytime fishing, evening pickups—the founding sketch. Long-term gameplay should let Bapak replace portions of that routine through earned convenience.

**Open:** Complexity of fishing controls, fish economy, catches vs fish farming, selling, tournament frequency, and how much of fishing is a full minigame for the MVP.
