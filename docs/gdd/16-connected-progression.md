# 16 — Connected Progression: Places, Assets, Family and Time

> **Status: PROPOSED for prototyping.** The creator agreed to try connected progression and document it. The governing fantasy and Bapak-only direct control are already canonical; exact progression paths, thresholds, rewards, timing, prices, and skill-tree UI are **not approved balance**.

## Thesis: unlock choices, not an XP ladder

Progression should answer: **"What can happen tomorrow that could not happen yesterday?"** A meaningful unlock adds an action, changes a repeated obligation, enables a new arrangement, or produces an enduring consequence. Flat +10% multipliers can be supporting modifiers but cannot carry a progression track alone.

The player pursues **usable freedom**: discretionary time while essentials are met and family members retain wellbeing and agency. More cash, a bigger house, and more fish are not universal victory conditions. Losing responsibilities through neglect is not counted as success.

## Connected resources

| Resource | Exists in the prototype? | Progression meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Time and responsibility coverage | Clock, pickup and fishing minutes: yes | Measure hours spent on obligations, paid work, travel, family, leisure, and unused time; compare consecutive days |
| Cash and debt | Wallet, fares, fish sales, bills, loan and risk games: yes | Liquidity, predictable income, cash buffer and repayment choices; requires a recurring economy |
| Access and capability | Vehicle, fuel, physical routes and pond: partial | Reach a location, carry passengers, use equipment, obtain permission or make an agreement |
| Relationships and autonomy | Yarn choices, NPC flags, schedules: partial | Change willingness, commitments, support, interests and recurring logistics without directly controlling Ibu/Anak |
| Security and resilience | Outstanding bills and distress-sale proof: partial | Absorb shocks, keep a home and avoid destructive short-term fixes; not a hidden morality score |

Do not introduce one universal XP currency that can directly buy Ibu's career, Anak's development, permission to fish on private property, or family affection.

## Fishing: location progression as a choice matrix

| Location / activity | Access | Distinct trade-off | Prototype priority |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Paid kolam pemancingan** | Session fee or ticket | Convenient, permitted and comparatively predictable; fee can erode catch profit | **First**: reuse the existing pond |
| **Public river / permitted bank** | Free public access; travel needed | Variable fish, weather, water, travel and different techniques; no automatic enforcement | **First**: add a second spot |
| **Restricted river stretch** | Requires explicit permission, otherwise prohibited | Attractive fishing vs visible enforcement/reputation risk; choices and exit paths must be legible | Later, optional |
| **Galatama / tournament** | Entry, skill and timing | Contest result, reputation and social stories; distinct rules from fish-selling ponds | Later |
| **Owned tambak / kolam budidaya** | Investment, land/lease and inputs | Growing, feeding, upkeep, harvesting and selling; **production business, not a paid fishing venue** | Later |

Fishing gear should unlock techniques and locations rather than only increase catch prices. Selling, keeping, gifting, eating, or displaying a catch could connect fishing to the household, inventory and social relationships. Fish access and rewards should not make ngojol useless.

**Important:** ordinary river fishing is not inherently illegal. A restricted zone must be intentionally authored, posted or communicated, with a plausible way to obtain permission; do not reward violating real-world regulations by default.

## Vehicle progression: a portfolio, not a speed ladder

- Starter **old Supra**: everyday mobility, family transport and some ojol; low capacity and maintenance exposure.
- **Repair/maintenance**: reliability, operating cost or service downtime choices; a practical alternative to replacement.
- **Second motorcycle**: can remain in service while the first is rented out; creates a rental-management opportunity **only if ownership and agreements are modeled**.
- **Used family car**: more seats, cargo capacity, family trips, greater costs and road/parking limitations.
- **More expensive vehicle**: specialist advantage, not universal superiority. Older vehicles remain useful.

Every major purchase needs a gameplay verb: reach, carry, rent, repair, transport, or coordinate. Fuel and wear need visible costs; renting away the sole household vehicle should meaningfully constrain school pickup and ojol access. Car ownership is not the obligatory ending.

## Home progression: convenience and social geography

- Starter **3-petak kontrakan** in a five-door communal courtyard: compact living, dense neighbor contact and limited privacy.
- **Functional furnishings/repairs**: for example, a study desk enables a quiet study routine, storage/refrigeration changes errands, and a covered parking space changes vehicle upkeep. These are proposed examples, not committed upgrades.
- **Move or expand**: gains privacy, space and security, but potentially loses walking-distance help, warung proximity and casual neighbor contact.
- Home stability and the option to repair relationships remain relevant independent of property value. No mandatory move-out ladder.

## Ibu: independent career and interests

Ibu's progression is a **career/life trajectory**, not a player-operated skill tree. Bapak can listen, support, plan logistics and negotiate agreements, not spend points to force promotions.

Candidate developments: role/shift change, supervisor responsibility, alternative work, social network, financial planning confidence. Each development has *two-sided effects*: salary timing or income may improve while pickup availability, household coordination or shared time changes. She retains independent spending, boundaries and choices. A difficulty preset may change household economic conditions, but cannot secretly reduce her competence or dignity.

## Anak: developmental agency and eventual endings

Anak's development affects ordinary gameplay **well before** any final ending. Candidate tracks are **learning, age-appropriate independence, friendships/interests, and bond with Bapak**. They are observed through events and decisions rather than a checklist or a +10% passive perk.

Example causal loop: Anak joins football practice → new pickup obligation → friendship and trusted carpool may develop over time → a recurring trip can become an agreed arrangement. Alternatively, shared fishing creates memories if Anak actually enjoys it. An older child might gain safe, age-appropriate independent commute choices, contingent on route, supervision and household agreement.

Bapak may support interests, share activities, make promises and keep them. He cannot directly train Anak into a predetermined ending; outcomes emerge from sustained environment, opportunities and relationships. School grades alone do not define wellbeing.

## Cross-system example

Bapak repairs the Supra → reliable commute permits extra ojol work → debt payments become manageable → Ibu has room to consider a shift change → school pickup plan is renegotiated → Anak joins an activity and develops a carpool → time previously spent commuting can go to family, fishing, or a small business. The chain should also work with setbacks and recovery.

Avoid a single dominant pathway: fishing-only leisure, balanced gig work, modest fish production, and optional asset/business ownership are different lifestyles. No tycoon escalation is required.

## First proof before building full trees

The [next milestone](../roadmap/milestone-01-economy-and-consequences.md) should make recurring money and the family-facing consequences of a decision work. Then prototype **two fishing access conditions**, **one vehicle improvement**, **one Ibu or Anak milestone**, and **one functional home improvement**. Demonstrate a changed daily choice in each. The [first-30-day sketch](../roadmap/first-30-days.md) is a pacing hypothesis, not a script or unlock schedule.

## Non-goals for this prototype

No generational simulation, full property market, elaborate tycoon economy, automatic child skill purchases, uncontrolled gambling progression, or unavoidable forced-divorce timeline.
