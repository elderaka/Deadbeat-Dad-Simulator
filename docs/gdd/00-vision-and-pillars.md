# 00 — Vision and Pillars

> **Status: CANONICAL** — Canonical = approved direction; Proposed = hypothesis to validate; Open = undecided. Numeric examples are not final balancing.

## High concept
**Deadbeat Dad Simulator / Bapak Simulator** is a comedic Indonesian suburban household-and-leisure game. Bapak wants to spend his life relaxing, especially fishing, while the household still functions and his family has room to flourish.

**The Art of Doing Absolutely Nothing** is the selected primary fantasy. The player is not rewarded for neglect: the joke is being improbably resourceful at removing obligations while still providing for the family.

## Four design pillars
1. **Household engineering:** solve recurring responsibilities via planning, relationships, investments and delegation.
2. **Leisure economy:** fishing, hobbies and side hustles are desirable active play, not compulsory unpaid labor.
3. **Living family:** partner and children have independent interests and trajectories; they are people, not upgrade trees.
4. **Kampung chaos:** routines collide with Indonesian neighborhood life to produce grounded comedy and anecdotes.

## North-star test
A mechanic belongs if it (a) helps the player earn autonomy/time, (b) creates meaningful family stories, or (c) generates entertaining systemic chaos. Ideally it does two.

## Tone and boundaries
- Affectionate parody of the *bapak-bapak* lifestyle, not ethnic caricature. No derogatory targeting of Sunda/Sundanese or other communities.
- **No real-money or monetized gambling.** The creator later accepted optional fictional judol using only non-purchasable in-game currency as a risky financial shortcut with negative long-run expectation. Do not sell wagers, cash-outs, or betting credits, and do not make betting necessary for progress. See [Risk Economy](15-risk-economy-and-neighbor-exploitation.md) and [ADR-0006 (risk)](../decisions/ADR-0006-risk-economy-social-exploitation.md).
- Family growth is optional and consensual narrative progression, not an obligatory score multiplier.
- Avoid chores for the sake of chores and mandatory daily punishments for doing the fun activities.

## Scope discipline

A deeply simulated family dynasty, a third-person driving experience, and online lobbies may be long-term ambitions; none is automatically a Day 1 implementation commitment.

**Evidence:** The creator explicitly selected *The Art of Doing Absolutely Nothing* as the player fantasy and deferred multiplayer to a future roadmap.
