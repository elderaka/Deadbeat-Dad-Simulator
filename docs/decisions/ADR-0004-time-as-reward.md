# ADR-0004 — Discretionary Time as the Main Reward

## Context
The selected fantasy implies that strategic effort should reduce recurring obligations rather than maximize raw productivity. Prior brainstorming recommended free time, a responsibility coverage index and durable conveniences.

## Proposal
Track reclaimed discretionary time as a progression indicator, gated by household wellbeing and fulfilled responsibilities. Financial improvement is supporting progression, not the only win condition.

## Constraints
A player who ignores their family to fish all day must not outperform a player who solved the household logistics. Free time alone cannot define success.

## Design candidates
- Show daily time allocation, reclaimed hours and obligations automatically covered.
- Use a household stability and responsibility coverage gate/weighting for any composite score.
- Keep the formula, thresholds and labels provisional until playtests.

## Status / action
This is strongly implied by [ADR-0001](ADR-0001-primary-fantasy.md) but **specific metric implementation is not accepted**. Validate the mechanic and user preference before treating the formula as canon.
