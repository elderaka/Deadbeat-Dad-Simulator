# ADR-0001 — The Art of Doing Absolutely Nothing

## Context
We considered three high-level player identities:
- The Accidental Tycoon — wealth and upgrading.
- The Unconventional Father — family stories and relationships.
- **The Art of Doing Absolutely Nothing** — clever household optimization so Bapak may enjoy leisure while family thrives.

## Decision
Creator explicitly selected **The Art of Doing Absolutely Nothing** as the primary fantasy and requested design around its core mechanics, progression and rewards.

## Implications
- Time freed from repetitive obligations is the primary feeling of improvement.
- The household must remain functional; neglect alone should not score as mastery.
- Fishing is important but not sufficient as a retention system.
- Relationships, durable convenience upgrades and systemic comedy must reinforce the fantasy.

## Not implied
This decision does *not* approve a particular camera, NPC AI technique, number of children, economy balance, engine, platform or ending.
