# ADR-0002 — Multiplayer Is a Future Roadmap Feature

## Context
The creator proposed lobby-style multiplayer where players could socialize and chat, with potential Roblox, Steam and Android directions. Multiplayer would substantially change scope and operational costs.

## Decision
Creator explicitly stated **"multiplayer is future roadmap"** and prioritized the gameplay loop, design and non-fishing hook.

## Consequences
- Single-player household mechanics are the proof of concept and MVP.
- Avoid prematurely adding lobby, networking, online accounts, hosted servers, cross-platform identity or text-chat dependencies.
- Preserve future extensibility when convenient, but do not sacrifice simple single-player development for speculative networking needs.

## Revisit when
The 7-day solo prototype is enjoyable and retention comes from life management, not just fish catches.
