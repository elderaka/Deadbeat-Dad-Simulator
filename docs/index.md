# Bapak Simulator — Design Vault

**North star:** The player tries to enjoy an absurdly relaxed life while helping their family thrive. The player gets better by removing repetitive obligations, not by stacking chores.

**Start here:**
- [Game Design Document](gdd/index.md) — structured game design, section by section

- [Journal](journal/index.md) — chronological brainstorms, work logs, and handoffs
- [Agent Protocol](agents/index.md) — how agents maintain context across compaction/delegation

## Document state definitions

| State | Meaning | Who may promote it? |
|---|---|---|
| **canonical** | Explicitly accepted intent or constraint | Creator decision, recorded as ADR when substantive |
| **proposed** | Working design candidate; safe to discuss and prototype | Must not be treated as fixed requirement |
| **open** | Unanswered trade-off or design question | Remains open until resolved |
| **living** | Index/process page maintained alongside docs | Contributors may update navigation |

**Warning to future agents:** examples and simulated budgets from brainstorming are not approved numerical balance. Fishing is a major leisure activity, but family systems—not fishing alone—must supply the game's hook.
