# Founding Brainstorm — 2026-11-02

## Original pitch (creator)
"Deadbeat Dad Simulator" is a comedic parody, not an attack on a demographic. Inspired by Indonesian factory-town / suburban atmosphere (e.g., Cikarang):
- Morning: deliver working wife to textile/factory shift and child to school.
- Daytime to evening: fishing.
- Evening: pick up wife and children.
- Upgrade vehicles and housing; support children's education, including potential boarding school.
- Additional children may increase living costs/complexity; eventual house/life simulator ambitions.
- **No gambling.**

## Design exploration
- Isometric life sim = easier family management / minigames; first-person = stronger "being Bapak" immersion. Hybrid and camera flexibility proposed, **not decided**.
- Player-only Bapak control vs whole-family direct control discussed. Bapak-only autonomous family suggested; **not explicitly settled**.
- Lobby multiplayer/chat suggested as amusing social chaos. Creator then clarified multiplayer belongs on **future roadmap**.
- Roblox versus standalone Steam/Android discussed; no final deployment decision.
- Creator challenged the key issue: what makes the game addictive beyond fishing, and whether raising a family as a deadbeat is compelling.

## Player fantasy candidates
1. Accidental Tycoon: optimize wealth.
2. Unconventional Father: emotional family growth.
3. **The Art of Doing Absolutely Nothing:** maintain a thriving family and earn the freedom to relax.

**Creator selected option 3 explicitly.** Recorded in [ADR-0001](../decisions/ADR-0001-primary-fantasy.md).

## Concepts from assistant's exploratory design (NOT all approved)
- Four pillars: household engineering, leisure economy, living family, kampung chaos.
- 12–15 real minutes per game-day as a pacing guess.
- Reclaim time by durable solutions (school pickup, chores, bill payment) with real ongoing costs.
- NPC schedule/trait/memory model; optional more children, education and generation outcomes.
- Multi-layer rewards: minute/day/week/long-term, photo chronicle and "Indeks Rebahan".
- Five example progression chapters and contextual random events.
- Seven-day MVP to test if management is fun without fishing loot addiction.

## Core tension
Must avoid (a) household micromanagement that turns fishing into a chore, (b) pure fishing grind where family is decorative, and (c) time-freedom metrics that reward neglect.

## Current unresolved creator decisions
Controls and autonomy, viewpoint, degree of realism, platform, audience/language and exact MVP scope. See [Open Questions](../gdd/12-open-questions.md).
